# UI ideas — 引言

## Overall concept

Make the introduction a visual argument: first show how a document moves through the Qing communication system, then show why these documents matter, why they are difficult to study, and why the platform is useful. The existing workflow dropdown can act as the section index.

## 1. 清代奏摺上諭 — interactive document-route map

This should be the signature interaction of the introduction.

### Proposed layout

- A large map or diagram occupies most of the screen.
- A route connects several stamped stages:
  `地方官員` → `夾` → `皇帝／硃批` → `軍機署` → `上諭` → `地方官員`.
- A small inset map can show the longer Taiwan-to-Beijing journey.
- A second inset can show the Forbidden City and identify the location of `軍機署`, if the source map supports that claim.
- Each stage has a real record, replica, or documentary image supplied by the researcher.

### Interaction

- Hover or tap a stamp to open a nearby evidence card.
- The card shows: stage name, document role, image, short explanation, and “查看來源” if a source reference is available.
- A “播放流程” button reveals the stages in order, but users can also explore them freely.
- A toggle can switch between `制度流程`, `地理路線`, and `文書實物` views.

### Important framing

Treat the route as a simplified explanatory model. The exact institutional sequence, the meaning of `夾`, and the relationship between record-making and the production or dispatch of an `上諭` should be checked against the chosen scholarship and archival evidence before presenting the route as universal. A small label such as `示意流程，詳情以史料及研究為準` would protect the research distinction.

## 2. 清代奏折上諭的研究價值

Use a three-layer communication diagram rather than another block of prose:

`地方資訊` → `奏摺` → `皇帝決策` → `硃批／上諭` → `地方回應`.

Animate one document at a time. Selecting a layer highlights the relevant part of the chain and reveals why the paired documents are valuable for reconstructing decision-making. Keep the wording focused on what the documents allow researchers to investigate, not on a claim that every surviving document gives a complete picture.

## 3. 研究清代奏折的主要困難 — three separate views

The repeated heading should become three clearly labelled difficulty cards, with a progress indicator such as `1 / 3`, `2 / 3`, `3 / 3`.

### A. 史料數量龐大

Show an image of `《欽定剿平三省邪匪方略》` beside a visual scale: volume stack, page counter, or long vertical scroll. The scale should communicate mass without inventing a fake exact visual ratio. A “放大細節” action can show how a single page is still dense and difficult to survey.

### B. 奏摺與上諭的通信關係複雜

Use the sample review-tool UI with one 奏摺 as the centre. Highlight:

- the passage responding to a previous `上諭` or `硃批`;
- the linked earlier document;
- the later `上諭` or later奏報 that responds to it.

The UI should show the relationship as a small three-document chain beside the original text. Clicking a link scrolls to the matching highlighted passage. The purpose is to make the research problem visible before introducing the platform solution.

### C. 同一事件有多個消息來源

Reuse the same 奏摺, but change the explanatory focus. Highlight the passage reporting the event, then show its stated or implied source of information. Beside it, show two or more source paths as separate lines, with different colours for `事件`, `消息來源`, and `研究者待核`.

Do not make the visual imply that every source relationship is already confirmed. Use labels such as `原文引文`, `AI 候選`, and `研究者確認` where appropriate.

## 4. 數位方法

Turn the current comparison table into a “problem → tool → researcher action” sequence. Each row becomes an expandable card:

- `史料數量龐大` → `AI／AI Skills` → 快速整理、提取、核對.
- `通信關係複雜` → `時間線／關係網絡` → 追蹤文書和事件的傳遞.

The researcher action should be visually prominent so the website does not frame AI as an automatic conclusion-maker.

## 5. 研究成果

Make this card a compact preview of the finished research platform rather than another static explanation.

- Place a looping GIF of the complete website interface in the main visual area.
- Show the GIF inside a browser-like frame with a short caption such as `從原文閱讀、AI 提取到圖表核驗`.
- Add pause, replay, and reduced-motion alternatives so the interaction is understandable without animation.
- Add three small callouts over selected moments in the GIF: `閱讀原文`, `核對 AI 結果`, and `查看事件鏈`.
- Keep a static first frame or screenshot available for slow connections and accessibility.

The GIF should show the actual research sequence: `原文 → AI 候選 → 人工核對 → 圖表節點`, with the researcher’s confirmation remaining visible as the crucial step.

## 6. 案例：林爽文事件

Use the existing three-card structure as a visual introduction to the case study. Each card should have one dominant historical image and one short explanatory caption.

### Card 1 — 戰爭場景

Show a Qing-dynasty drawing depicting a scene from the war. Use the image as the background or large left-hand visual, with a small caption identifying the image, collection, and citation. The text should explain that the drawing establishes the historical setting, while the platform investigates the documentary communication surrounding the event.

### Card 2 — 資訊傳遞路線

Show the map depicting how information was sent from Taiwan to Beijing. Use a highlighted route from Taiwan to the imperial centre, with a small legend for `地方奏報`, `傳遞路線`, and `北京接收／處理`. If the map is adapted from later scholarship, label it clearly as a scholarly reconstruction and include the source.

### Card 3 — 研究材料

Show the covers of `《明清臺灣檔案彙編》（臺灣史料集成編輯委員會編）` and `《天地會檔案彙編》（中國人民大學清史研究所、中國第一歷史檔案館編）` side by side. Add a short caption explaining that these compilations provide the documentary material used in the demonstration. Keep the two titles visually separate so the reader can identify which source is which.

The card sequence should move from `戰爭場景` → `資訊如何傳遞` → `研究者使用哪些材料`, ending with a clear `進入案例` action.

## Asset checklist

- Scholar-designed Taiwan-to-Beijing route map, with permission and citation.
- Forbidden City map identifying `軍機署`, with source and uncertainty note if needed.
- Real records or replicas for every route stage.
- Image of `《欽定剿平三省邪匪方略》`.
- One sample 奏摺 screenshot from the review tool.
- Highlighted examples for `回應上諭` and `事件／消息來源`.
- Final full-interface GIF of the website, plus a static first frame.
- Qing-dynasty war-scene drawing and its catalogue/source information.
- Taiwan-to-Beijing information-route map and its scholarly source.
- Covers of `《明清臺灣檔案彙編》` and `《天地會檔案彙編》`, with image permissions and bibliographic details.

## Open design decisions

- Whether the route map is geographic, institutional, or a switchable combination of both.
- Whether image cards open on hover, click, or both; click is safer for touch screens.
- Whether the same 奏摺 screenshot is reused in both difficulty sections or shown as two focused states.
