# UI ideas — 第二部分

## Purpose

Show the research workflow as a chain with checkpoints, not as a black-box AI pipeline.

## Main visual

Use a horizontal or gently vertical workflow:

`輸入結構化資料` → `AI Skills` → `載入分析結果` → `研究者審閱` → `加入圖表`.

Each step should have a document thumbnail, a short input/output description, and a visible checkpoint. Selecting a step opens a larger panel without leaving the tab.

## Suggested demonstrations

- For `輸入結構化資料`, show the same document in source form and structured fields.
- For `總結文書`, show a collapsed summary expanding into section labels, while keeping a link to the full text.
- For `重建通信關係`, show two candidate documents connected by date, quotation, and author evidence.
- For `辨識奏摺所回應的上諭`, show the quoted phrase and the candidate `上諭` side by side.
- For `辨識上諭所回應的奏摺`, reverse the direction so students see that the relationship can be investigated from either document.
- For `研究者審閱`, show three explicit choices: `接受`, `修改`, `拒絕`.
- For `結果載入至圖表`, animate only a confirmed result becoming a node and connection.

## Important interaction rule

Do not auto-advance from AI output to chart output. Require a visible “研究者確認後加入圖表” step. This communicates the project’s provisional-review principle.

## Possible supporting elements

- A top progress bar showing the current stage.
- A “查看原文” drawer available at every AI stage.
- A small audit trail showing what was suggested, changed, and confirmed.
- A toggle between `流程視圖` and `實際介面視圖`.
