# UI ideas — 主頁

## Purpose

The home tab should establish the project in one glance: this is a research platform for reading, checking, connecting, and reusing Qing documents. It should feel like an invitation into a research space rather than a software dashboard.

## Main composition

- A quiet full-screen title area with `清代奏摺與上諭分析平台` as the visual anchor.
- A short subtitle explaining the research subject and the example case `林爽文事件`.
- One strong “開始探索” action that opens `引言`.
- A secondary “直接查看平台” action that opens `第一部分`.
- A small visual preview of the platform’s four layers: 原始史料、AI 分析、時間與關係圖表、研究者審閱.

## Possible interaction

Use a slow, horizontal flow of four document-shaped layers. Hovering or tapping each layer reveals one sentence about its role. The interaction should remain optional: the title and two buttons must still make sense without animation.

## Visual direction

Use paper, ink, vermilion, and muted archive colours. A faint document texture or seal-like mark can give the page historical character, but the page should not look like a museum reconstruction. Keep the navigation and settings control visible and calm.

## Content to prepare

- Final one-sentence project description.
- One approved image or cropped detail that represents the source material.
- Exact labels for the four platform areas.
- A clear distinction between AI suggestions and researcher-confirmed results.

## Questions to resolve

- Is the home page primarily for students, competition judges, or researchers returning to the tool?
- Should the first action lead to the historical explanation or to the working platform?
- Do we want a real source image here, or should real images begin in the `引言` tab?
