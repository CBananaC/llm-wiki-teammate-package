# 2nd Material & FYP — organized research context

This folder is a reorganization of the research collection as of 2026-07-23.
The folder map below preserves the intended organization of the source
collection. In the current checkout, the visible material directories are
`(2)二手研究`, `(3)FYP寫作與研究設計`, `(4)地圖與研究工具`, and `FYP`; the
older legacy-container names in the map are not assumed to be present until
they appear in the tree.

## Folder map

- `(1)一手史料` — structured 奏摺／上諭 data, selected source texts, quotations, date-delay calculations, and source-analysis tables.
- `(2)二手研究` — secondary notes grouped by the theme they address:
  - 林爽文事件、戰爭進程、戰略、軍需與跨海投送
  - 清代臺灣治理、人口、地方社會、綠營、武力、海防與海運
  - 軍機處、中央決策、檔案、方略、奏摺制度與驛傳
  - 新政治史、動態制度史，以及北宋軍事指揮的比較材料
- `(3)FYP寫作與研究設計` — research questions, introduction, outline, background, research review, draft chapters, and information-delay notes.
- `(4)地圖與研究工具` — battle maps, historical maps, place-name notes, coordinates, KMZ, and GIS-related files.
- `(5)產生文字匯出` — generated TXT bundles kept as an archive, not treated as independent source copies.
- `(6)待確認` — preserved files that should be reviewed before any future consolidation.
- `(8)舊資料夾、系統檔_一手史料`, `(9)舊資料夾、系統檔_二手研究`, and `(10)舊資料夾、系統檔` — preserved legacy containers holding only system metadata.

## Review policy

- `(1)重複與變體文件` contains the exact duplicate DOCX files from the old `Docx Note` mirror, plus the exact `copy` files and the formatting-only `正文_indented_titles.docx` variant.
- `(2)草稿版本文件` contains the two non-identical older `copy` drafts of the war-process and pre-uprising Taiwan notes.
- `(3)重複非文件` contains the duplicate text-export set, duplicate map/GIS assets, and the duplicate selected-text JSON file.
- `(9)暫存與系統檔` contains Word lock files and `.DS_Store` metadata. These were retained because the instruction was to remove nothing.

The canonical copies were placed in the thematic folders; the review copies retain their old relative paths where that helps identify their origin.

## How this folder informs the system and teaching website

This folder is contextual research material rather than a second canonical
source corpus. Its main contribution to the review system and the future
student-facing website is the explanation of why the system follows official
document movement, timing, relationships, and spatial context.

- `(2)二手研究/(1)林爽文事件與戰爭進程` supplies background on the event,
  battle stages, strategy, military supply, cross-sea mobilisation, and the
  use of maps/GIS in reconstructing the war.
- `(2)二手研究/(2)清代臺灣治理、社會與軍事制度` provides context on Qing
  governance in Taiwan, local society and population, Green Standard forces,
  maritime defence, and the mobilisation of official and local forces.
- `(2)二手研究/(3)中央制度、軍機處與文書傳遞` explains the institutional
  setting for memorials, imperial rescripts, the Grand Council, archives,
  方略, courier routes, and wartime information flow.
- `(2)二手研究/(4)新政治史與比較研究` supports a dynamic-institutional
  approach: study how rules and documents operated in practice, not only how
  institutions were described in formal regulations.
- `(3)FYP寫作與研究設計` records the working research question, draft
  chapters, literature review, information-delay calculations, and the
  proposed comparison of frontline, Fujian, and Beijing information positions.
- `(4)地圖與研究工具` supports the spatial side of the explanation through
  historical maps, place-name notes, coordinates, KMZ data, and GIS material.

The notes include working summaries, OCR-derived text, copied excerpts, and
references that may still need page-level checking. Before using any claim,
quotation, image, map, or citation in the public teaching website, verify it
against the underlying publication or source record. The canonical evidence
and reviewed outputs remain under `review-tools/` and `review-tools/shared data/`.
